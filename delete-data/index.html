Delete Your Data

A simple explanation of how your information stays in your hands.

Your information stays with you

Bloom stores your reflections, milestones, mood check-ins, and pregnancy journey only on your device. Bloom does not create online accounts or store your information on external servers.

Delete reflections at any time

You can remove individual reflections, milestones, and saved moments directly inside the app whenever you choose.

Clear everything from your device

You can remove all Bloom data at any time by:

  • navigating to the Sanctuary tab and selecting ‘Clear my journey’
  • clearing the app’s storage from your device settings
  • or uninstalling Bloom entirely

Once removed, your data cannot be recovered.

Exported PDFs stay on your device

Memory book PDFs created with Bloom are generated locally on your phone. If you later choose to remove them, they can be deleted directly from your device’s files or downloads folder.

No server-side copies

Bloom does not keep copies of your reflections, exports, or personal information. Because your data is never uploaded to Bloom servers, there is nothing for Bloom to remotely delete, share, or recover.
