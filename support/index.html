Does Bloom require an account?

No — Bloom works entirely offline. There is no sign-up, no login, and no account to manage. Open the app, and it is yours.

Where are my reflections stored?

Your reflections remain on your device. They are not uploaded, synced, or stored on any server. Only you have access to what you write.

Does Bloom provide medical advice?

Bloom is a gentle emotional companion, not a medical resource. It is here to hold space for your reflections and quiet moments — not to offer clinical guidance. Please speak with a healthcare provider for any medical concerns.

Bloom was designed to work quietly, privately, and entirely offline-first.
